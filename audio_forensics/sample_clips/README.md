## Critical Real-World Generalization & Audit Caveat

> [!WARNING]
> **Training Set Generalization Gap**:
> The `garystafford/wav2vec2-deepfake-voice-detector` model was pre-trained on a compact dataset (~1,866 audio samples).
> While the model's reported training/validation metrics are high (0.998 AUC), real-world synthetic speech generated by modern voice clone models (**ElevenLabs, Bark, XTTS v2, CosyVoice, OpenAI Voice Engine**) exhibits domain shift that can cause false negatives or score compression.

### Minimum Production Deployment Bar:
1. **Equal Error Rate (EER) Evaluation**: Benchmark the model against a broader standard dataset (e.g. ASVspoof 2019/2021 LA or In-the-Wild Audio Dataset) to obtain verified EER numbers.
2. **Hold-out 20–30 Clip Manual Audit**: Before deploying to production, manually run 20 to 30 voice samples generated by current state-of-the-art TTS engines not present in the training data to calibrate detection thresholds.
3. **Cross-Modal Consistency Guard**: Do not rely on `s_audio` / `audio_score` alone; always combine audio scores with `text_forensics` entropy/burstiness signals and cross-modal transcript consistency checks in `fusion.py`.
